This video presents GD2P, a geometry-aware framework for nonprehensile pushing and pulling with dexterous robotic hands. The method combines pose optimization, a geometry-conditioned diffusion model, simulation validation, and motion planning to generate effective pre-contact hand poses. We evaluate the approach on 14 objects with extensive real-world experiments totaling over 840 trials. Results show strong performance compared to baselines, support for multi-step pushing, and generalization across different hand designs. The video demonstrates robust object transport without grasping and highlights the potential of geometry-aware nonprehensile manipulation.
